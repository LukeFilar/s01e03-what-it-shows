#4471 · three problems, not one

owner support deskproposedreviewed 2026-11-01 · 0 days ago

#4471 says one issue, one fix queued. That is wrong. The 412 reports merged into it hold three problems, and one fix does not close them.

Merging on subject line alone (decision 0001) put reports with the same subject but different causes into one ticket. Intake's browser guess (intakeTag) is not a safe way to tell them apart: it disagrees with the real cause on 38 reports.

The three problems

problemreports
Shared parser reads dates month first374
booking-v3 form hints MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants33
Date lands one day off (not a month/day flip)5
merged into #4471412
  1. Parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get flipped dates in the app, in email and through the booking-v2 form. This is the bug #4471 describes.
  2. Form hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) type month first because the form tells them to, and the form then reads the date their way. The parser fix does not fix this.
  3. One day off. A few reports describe a date that is one day off, not a swapped day and month: Lisbon and Toronto got different days for one meeting. Their bodies are short, so a time zone cause is a guess. They need their own look before anyone calls them fixed.

What the desk code says

causes.ts places each report by tenant locale and booking form. It files the one-day reports under parser and footnotes them.
